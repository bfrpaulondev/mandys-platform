Public market anchors checked on 2026-08-17 place Square Restaurants around £69/location/month in the UK, around C$60 in Canada and around A$129 in Australia; public Soft Restaurant pricing provides Mexican anchors roughly from MX$699 through MX$1,599 depending on package. Mandy's should compete on consolidated functionality rather than pure undercutting.

## Spanish-speaking Latin America
Do not reuse Spain EUR pricing. Validate each local band from restaurant/POS SaaS prices, hospitality payroll, restaurant ticket/revenue, taxes/payment-provider costs, support/localization cost and currency volatility. Recommended rollout after Mexico: Colombia, Chile, Argentina and Peru.

## Seat-billing rule
- only active organization members count;
- pending invitations do not count;
- public customers/guests never count;
- removed staff stop counting next billing period;
- each plan includes enough staff to avoid seat micromanagement;
- Multi allowance is per location, with a future group discount curve.

## Markets still needing dedicated bands before public pricing
Ireland, New Zealand, Singapore, UAE, South Africa, Japan, South Korea and remaining Latin-American currencies. Until validated, the product may support the locale technically but must not silently assign another country's commercial price.
