## Market anchor notes

- UK/Canada/Australia use local Square Restaurant pricing as mainstream anchors, with Mandy's differentiated by a broader integrated operations stack.
- Ireland has a particularly useful seat-pricing signal: Square Restaurants Plus is around €69/location while Square Shifts includes the first five staff and then charges a small per-person amount, matching Mandy's location-first model.
- Japan uses Square Restaurants and Square Shifts as local price/seat anchors.
- Singapore uses locally advertised restaurant POS bands such as Restologia and Atlas.
- New Zealand uses public bands from SalesPoint and SmakSpace.
- UAE uses local restaurant SaaS/POS bands such as BrixPOS and HoreX rather than EUR conversion.
- South Africa uses local software bands plus low per-employee scheduling prices as a seat sanity check.
- Peru uses local restaurant/POS bands from entry packages around S/89 through more complete per-location offerings above S/200.

## Spanish-speaking Latin America

Do not reuse Spain EUR pricing. Peru now has a dedicated PEN candidate band. Colombia, Chile and Argentina remain next; Argentina needs an explicit price-review cadence because nominal local-currency pricing can age quickly.

## Seat-billing rule

- only active organization members count;
- pending invitations do not count;
- public customers/guests never count;
- removed staff stop counting next billing period;
- each plan includes enough staff to avoid seat micromanagement;
- Multi allowance is per location, with a future group discount curve.

## Remaining markets before public pricing

South Korea and additional Latin-American/Asian/African markets still require a dedicated validated band. Technical locale support never silently assigns another country's commercial price.
