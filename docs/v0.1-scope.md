# Mandy's V0.1

## Included

- Multi-tenant organization model.
- `pt-PT`, `pt-BR`, `en` and `es` as first-class locales.
- User language preference and tenant storefront locale configuration.
- Mandy's Minimal theme and theme entitlement model.
- Installable responsive backoffice PWA.
- Restaurant and location profile.
- Opening hours.
- Menu CMS with translated names/descriptions and allergens.
- Dining areas and tables.
- Customer CRM foundation and history.
- Reservations, booking policies, special days and waitlist.
- Group/event enquiry pipeline.
- Direct takeaway orders with kitchen status flow and public tracking.
- In-app notifications.
- Stock foundation with ingredients, suppliers, recipes, movements and average costs.
- Operational Insights across reservations, customers, events, orders, menu and stock.
- Audit/activity viewer.
- Team and access management.
- SaaS plan/subscription foundation with private, unpublished pricing.
- Public storefront and direct reservation/takeaway/event experiences.
- Deployed browser E2E readiness checks against public Storefront and Backoffice targets.

## Designed or still incomplete for commercial production

- Online card payments for restaurant customer orders.
- SaaS checkout, subscription webhooks and customer billing portal.
- External transactional email/SMS delivery.
- Tenant export, deletion and configurable retention workflows.
- Managed media upload pipeline for restaurant/menu assets.
- Loyalty.
- Mandy's AI.
- Multi-location operational dashboards and group administration.
- Offline private data mutations.

These capabilities remain separate module entitlements so they can be added without changing the core tenant model.
