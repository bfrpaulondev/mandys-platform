# Mandy's V0.1

## Included and test-ready

- Multi-tenant organization model with transaction-scoped tenant context and PostgreSQL RLS.
- `pt-PT`, `pt-BR`, `en` and `es` as first-class locales.
- User language preference and tenant storefront locale configuration.
- Mandy's Minimal theme and theme entitlement model.
- Installable responsive Backoffice PWA.
- International restaurant/location onboarding with country, currency and timezone.
- Restaurant profile, branding, opening hours, dining areas and tables.
- Menu CMS with translations, allergens, edit/delete/reorder flows, publication and signed-media integration surface.
- Customer CRM foundation, consent and operational history.
- Reservations, booking policies, special days and waitlist.
- Group/event enquiry pipeline.
- Direct takeaway orders with kitchen status flow and public tracking.
- In-app notifications.
- Stock with ingredients, suppliers, recipes, movements, average costs and low-stock monitoring.
- Operational Insights across reservations, customers, events, orders, menu and stock.
- Audit/activity viewer.
- Team and access management with owner, manager, reception, kitchen, staff, marketing and accounting roles.
- Role-aware Backoffice navigation and least-privilege runtime enforcement.
- SaaS plan/subscription foundation and country-specific private pricing.
- Stripe checkout, webhook and customer-portal foundations in fail-closed mode.
- Transactional email outbox/worker with idempotency, tenant kill switch and fail-closed provider activation.
- Tenant-configurable retention policy, owner-only export and atomic tenant deletion.
- Managed signed-media upload foundation with fail-closed Cloudinary activation.
- Public storefront and direct reservation/takeaway/event experiences.
- Versioned live-readiness checks, critical Edge-runtime monitoring and deployed Chromium E2E.
- Disposable authenticated lifecycle, cross-tenant isolation and operational-role authorization tests.

## Deliberately inactive during internal QA

The following integrations are implemented to the point where product testing does not require them, but remain blocked until commercial/provider configuration is explicitly approved:

- live SaaS charging and public plan prices;
- Stripe fiscal/commercial activation;
- real transactional email delivery and sender-domain activation;
- Cloudinary server-side production secret/preset activation;
- online card payment for restaurant customer orders.

## Later product expansion

- Loyalty.
- Mandy's AI.
- Multi-location operational dashboards and group administration.
- Offline private data mutations.
- Broader delivery/payment-provider integrations.

These capabilities remain separable through module entitlements and provider safety switches so the core tenant model does not need to change.
